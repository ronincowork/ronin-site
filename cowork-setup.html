06

Ronin Services optional

Base Cowork remains free and local whether or not you add Services.

What leaves this machine: If selected, Save sends your email address, the Services terms version, and this install’s activation request to Ronin. Ronin sends a confirmation email and issues the entitlement after you confirm. Declining Services sends none of this.

Save opens Cowork immediately. Each chosen agent starts in its own visible tile.
