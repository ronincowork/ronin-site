The home management team.

A House Manager that knows how your household runs — and switches between the week's meals, the shopping list, the leaking gutter, and the monthly budget without losing the thread of any of them.

family role: House Manager — who the agent remains, week after week

session task: meal planning — what it is doing at this moment

House Manager is a role you define in your own Ronin — the same way the built-in Developer role is defined, but yours. See Make Ronin your own.

01

The role: one manager for the whole house

The House Manager's briefing holds what is always true: who lives here, what the routines are, which stores you use, what the house itself needs. Because the role is fixed, every conversation starts from the same understanding of your household — you never re-explain the basics to plan one week's dinners.

02

The tasks: four kinds of errand

Each task carries its own working notes — where the recipe list lives, how the maintenance log is kept, what the budget categories are. The manager switches task as the work moves:

meal planning Plan the week's meals around who is home and what is in the fridge.
grocery runs Turn the meal plan into a shopping list, organized the way you shop.
maintenance scheduling Track what the house needs — filters, gutters, servicing — and when.
bills & budget Keep the monthly picture current and flag what looks off.
03

Why the split matters

Household work is many small, different jobs sharing one context. The role holds the shared context; each task brings only what that errand needs. Ask about dinner and you get the meal planner; ask about the furnace and the same manager picks up the maintenance notes instead — no single overloaded prompt trying to be everything at once.
