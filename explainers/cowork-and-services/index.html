Explainers / Ronin Cowork and Services

How much coordination do I want?

Start bare. Add only what helps.

Ronin can be an always-on terminal around your existing Agent, or it can add progressively more structured coordination. Beta Ronin Services remain a separate axis.

Four coordination choices

Start bare, then add the layers that help.

Starting point

Bare Agent

  • Claude, Codex, or another CLI
  • always-on tmux terminal
  • no reading, Library, work record, or Routines
Optional first layer

Cowork floor

  • launch and session identity
  • Campaign and Team resolution
  • vocabulary, shelf map, and birth receipt
Optional Routine

Ronin Base

  • show files and keep documents
  • fork Agents and send messages
  • team wipeboard and work records
Optional Routine

Managed file coordination

  • separate worktrees and desk status
  • hand-in and lead integration
  • receipts and Git safeguards

Bare metal remains a complete choice. Choosing Ronin Cowork adds its floor; Base and managed file coordination add further capabilities only when selected.

A separate beta axis

Ronin Services are in beta today.

Ronin Cowork works fully without them. It is early days for Services: capabilities are still becoming ready, and readable transcripts are in development rather than live today.

Beta installed capabilities

Ronin Services

  • live work records
  • readable transcripts in development
  • Stats and Voice
  • gbrain integrations

Sharing your email is optional. It registers your interest, keeps you part of the Ronin community as it grows, and lets ready capabilities reach you as they land.

Four different facts

Selected is not the same as available.

  • EnabledDid Campaign or Team configuration select the Routine?
  • InstalledAre its local or service parts present on this machine?
  • ConnectedWas an MCP or other live connection delivered?
  • ApplicableDoes the present repository or situation use this behaviour?

An enabled but unavailable Routine does not block Agent birth. The Agent opens; the unavailable behaviour is reported as not delivered.

What the map does not claim

These choices equip an Agent; they do not control or sandbox it. Managed file coordination organizes file work rather than taking authority over the Agent. Services do not replace the coworkspace, and provider accounts and model data remain governed by the providers you choose.
