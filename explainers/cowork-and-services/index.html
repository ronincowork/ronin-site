Explainers / Cowork and Services

What is included, and what is additional?

A lightweight base. Control when you want it. Services when you need more.

Ronin's open coworkspace runs on its own. Routines shape how new Agents work; additional Services provide a separate set of installed capabilities.

The map

Three useful labels, two different axes.

Selectable Routine

Ronin Base

  • macros and documents
  • work records
  • session coordination
Selectable Routine

Ronin Control

  • managed repository desks
  • hand-in and team promotion
  • receipts and Git guards
Additional capabilities

Services

  • live work records
  • readable transcripts
  • Stats and Voice
  • gbrain integrations

Base and Control are Routine choices. Services are installed separately. Calling both “optional” does not make them the same kind of switch.

Four different facts

Selected is not the same as available.

  • EnabledCampaign or Team configuration selected the Routine.
  • InstalledThe relevant local or service parts are present on this machine.
  • Connected and applicableA live connection was delivered, and this repository or situation uses the behaviour.

An enabled but unavailable Routine does not block Agent birth. The Agent opens; the unavailable behaviour is reported as not delivered.

What the map does not claim

Routine switches are enablement, not security isolation. Services do not replace the base coworkspace, and the base does not silently claim a Services capability. Provider accounts and model data remain governed by the providers you choose.
