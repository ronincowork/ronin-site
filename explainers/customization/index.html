Customization

Make Ronin your own.

The Developer team and the fitness team are starting points, not the menu. Roles, tasks, macros, SOPs, and skins are all definitions you can add to, replace, or hide — so your Ronin works the way you do.

You do not edit configuration by hand.

You tell your agent. Describe the role, task, macro, SOP, or skin you want to any Ronin session; it drafts the definition, shows it to you, and writes it once you approve. Customizing Ronin is a conversation.

Ask the session you are in

Hand the customization to a session

Copy this into any Ronin agent session. The agent reads how customization works on your actual install, drafts your definition, and waits for your approval before writing anything. 

Help me customize this Ronin with my own family role, session task, macro, SOP, or skin.

Work on the machine where this session is running, and propose before you write.

1. Read this install's own guidance first: the README files in its catalogs and its shadowing documentation.
2. Ask me what I want — who the agent should be (a family role), what work it should do (its session tasks), or which macro, SOP, or skin I want changed — and how it should behave.
3. Draft each definition and show it to me in full before writing anything.
4. Write approved definitions only into my own catalogs store, never into Ronin's shipped files.
5. Confirm the result appears where it should (the launcher for roles and tasks), and tell me how to use it.
6. Do not change or remove any existing definition unless I explicitly ask.
01

What you can customize

Everything an agent session is shaped by is a definition, and every kind has the same open door:

family roles Who an agent is — a Head Coach, a House Manager, whatever hat your work needs.
session tasks The kinds of work on a role's shelf, each with its own briefing.
macros The shortcuts agents use to communicate and coordinate.
SOPs & skins How work is done in your house, and how your coworkspace looks.
02

Yours and ours

Ronin ships with a working set — the Developer role, its tasks, the stock macros. Your definitions live separately, in your own store, and there are three moves:

  • Add a new definition under a new name — a role or task of your own.
  • Replace one of ours whole, by defining your own version of it.
  • Hide one of ours you never use, without deleting anything.

Because yours live in your own store, a Ronin update refreshes the stock set and never touches your definitions. Your Head Coach survives every upgrade.

03

Start from a team, keep what fits

The easiest customization is an edit: take a team you have seen, keep the shape, and change the words — your training history in the coach's briefing, your stores in the house manager's notes, your review standards in the developer's posture. The teams are worked examples of definitions you already have permission to change.
