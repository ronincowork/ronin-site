Your owner stores live outside Ronin's repositories, so an update does not overwrite them. A definition with the same name shadows Ronin's stock definition; a new name extends the available material.

The honest shadowing tradeoff

Your shadow continues to win after an update. That preserves your customization, but later improvements to the stock definition you replaced do not automatically enter your version. Ronin marks the material as yours so the distinction remains visible.
