Why Ronin Hosted

A VM without the hassle.

You get the machine. Ronin gives you the interface. Start working with agents immediately instead of setting up a server, private access, sessions and a workspace yourself.

01
Ready

Your own VM, out of the box.

Dedicated storage, RAM and compute. Ronin is installed, authenticated and ready when you arrive.

02
Ronin interface

Work the agent—or manage the project.

Talk directly to the agent responsible for the work, or manage the project through work items on Jira.

03
No lock-in

Your agents. Your subscriptions.

Use Claude, Codex and other agents together in one common coworking space. Try new models as they reach the market without rebuilding where you work.

04
Always running

Close your laptop. The work continues.

Your VM and its agent sessions keep running. Come back through Ronin wherever you are.

Two ways to work

Direct when you want. Managed when you don't.

Ronin does not hide your agents behind another agent. You can speak to the person doing the work or follow the work itself.

DIRECT

Talk to the responsible agent.

Open the session, ask a question, change direction or keep working together in the native conversation.

  • Visible, separate agents
  • Claude, Codex and others
  • Your provider accounts
PROJECT MANAGED

Manage through work items.

See what is in progress, handed in or waiting without interrupting every agent for a status report.

  • Project Work Items
  • Optional Jira application integration
  • Roster, work records and reviewed handoffs

Start now. Add your subscriptions later.

Ronin starter credits let the first agents work when you arrive. Add your own provider subscriptions when you are ready.
