Template library

A team, and everything it reads, as one download.

A template bundle holds a team template, the agent templates beside it, and the SOPs, Routines, macros and tools they name. This page is the shelf: what is on it and what each one holds. The download happens inside your Ronin coworkspace — press Check the library on the Templates card, and Ronin fetches this shelf, shows you the plan, and installs what you choose into your own stores.

How a bundle gets in

  1. Open the Campaign page in your Ronin coworkspace and place Templates in a workspace.
  2. Press Check the library. Ronin asks this site for the shelf — only when you press, never on a timer — and shows the same bundles you see here.
  3. Pick one. Ronin shows the plan first: every file, the shelf it lands on, and whether it is new, replaces a shipped copy, or would replace one of yours. Nothing is written yet.
  4. Press Install. Every copy lands in your own stores on your machine, where an update never touches it. A file you already own is kept unless you choose the second button.

There is no file to move from the internet to your computer to the app: the app does the fetching, checks each bundle against the hash this shelf publishes, and a tool in a bundle never replaces one of Ronin's own.

Have a team worth sharing?

Any team template on your machine, with the books and Routines you gave it, packs into the same shape from its card on the Templates page — for a shelf of your own, or for this one.
