How one gets into your Ronin

Not from here. Open the Campaign page in your Ronin coworkspace, place Templates in a workspace, press Check the library, and Ronin shows this same shelf. Pick one; Ronin shows what it would write into your stores and writes nothing until you press Install. There is no file to move from the internet to your computer to the app.
