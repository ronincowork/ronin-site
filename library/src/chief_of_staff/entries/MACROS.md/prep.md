## prep
- **class:** session_macro.workflow
- **label:** +prep:
- **blurb:** One page per upcoming meeting — who, why now, last time, open threads, three questions worth asking.
The meeting-prep lane's procedure; the book is `sops:chief_of_staff`. Read `calendar/`
and `out/`; last time's notes and the open threads come from what other lanes wrote, not
from memory. One page per meeting, never longer.

Params: `meeting` (optional — one meeting by name; bare `+prep:` is every meeting in the next working day).

| # | Action | With |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | read-work-record | earlier `out/prep-*` and `out/triage-*` documents that mention the people involved |
| 2 | list-doc | write `out/prep-<date>-<meeting>.md` per meeting and list each |
| 3 | report-outcome | the meetings prepared, one line each, and where the pages are |
