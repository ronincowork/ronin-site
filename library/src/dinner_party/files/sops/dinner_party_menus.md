# dinner_party_menus — the house menu book

> Installed from the template library. Your own copy in the sops store (`ronin-store sops`
> → `dinner_party_menus.md`) replaces this file whole. Edit it: it is a starting book, and
> the point of it is that yours differs.
> **Voice: agent.** Written for the menu-and-shopping session, to follow itself.

Three menus that have worked, and the rules that made them work. Pick one, adapt it to the
guest list, and turn it into one shopping list in the order the shop is laid out.

## Rules before menus

- Ask for allergies and dislikes before choosing anything; write them at the top of the plan.
- One course that is finished before the guests arrive, one that only needs heating, and at
  most one that is cooked while they are there.
- Nothing new on the night. A dish the host has not cooked before is for another evening.
- The shopping list is one list, grouped as the shop is: produce, dairy, meat and fish, dry
  goods, drink. Quantities per head, then multiplied, then rounded up.

## Menu 1 — the reliable one (six to eight)

- To start: roasted peppers with anchovy and good bread (done that morning).
- Main: a slow braise — beef or mushroom — with a green salad and boiled potatoes.
- To finish: a tart bought from a good baker, with cream. Nobody minds.

## Menu 2 — the light one (four to six)

- To start: a chilled soup, served in cups.
- Main: whole baked fish with lemon and herbs, green beans, new potatoes.
- To finish: fruit, cheese, and one thing sweet.

## Menu 3 — the vegetarian one (any number)

- To start: a plate of small things — olives, hummus, roasted vegetables, flatbread.
- Main: a large baked dish that comes to the table whole — a lasagne, a gratin, a moussaka.
- To finish: a cake made the day before.

## Timing

Work backwards from the first course at the table. Write the times on the plan as
"done by", never "start at": a dish that is done by 19:00 can be started whenever the
cook likes.
