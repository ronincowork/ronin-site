# newsletter_style — how this house writes an issue

> Installed from the template library. Your own copy in the sops store (`ronin-store sops`
> → `newsletter_style.md`) replaces this file whole. Replace it with your own house style;
> this one is a default that reads well until you do.
> **Voice: agent.** Written for the sessions that write and edit, to follow themselves.

## The issue

- **Subject line:** under 50 characters, says what is in the issue, no exclamation marks.
- **Opening:** two sentences at most. What this issue is about and why now.
- **Items:** three to six. Each is a bold first line that stands alone, then two to four
  sentences. One idea per item. A link goes on its own line, after the sentences.
- **Closing:** one line, one ask. Reply, forward, or nothing — never three asks.

## The sentences

- Plain words. "Use", not "utilise"; "start", not "commence".
- Numbers as digits, with the unit. "3 days", not "three days".
- No claim without a source the checker can open. A number without a source is cut, not
  softened.
- Say who did the thing. "We shipped", "Maria wrote", never "it was decided".
- Contractions are fine. Emoji are not.

## The check

Every claim, number, name and link goes to the fact checker with its source beside it. The
checker marks each one confirmed or not. An unconfirmed claim is cut by the editor, not
argued over by the writer.

## The send

A test send to the owner first, read on a phone. Subject, preview text and the first item
are what most people will see; those three are checked twice.
