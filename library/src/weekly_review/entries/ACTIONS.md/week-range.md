## week-range — the Monday and Sunday of a week, measured
`action_kind: mechanical` — run it, don't deliberate.
> Tool: `tejun-week [date]` → `WEEK 2026-W36  2026-08-31 .. 2026-09-06`
Dates are measured, never worked out by hand: a review headed with the wrong week is
filed under the wrong week. One call, copy the line into the heading.
