# Weekly Review
- **label:** Weekly Review
- **art:** 🗓
- **blurb:** A week looked back on, and the next one shaped.
- **order:** 45
- **kinds:** work, personal
- **objective:** Look back over the week's work records and documents, say plainly what moved and what stalled, and shape next week into a short list the owner agrees to.
- **behaviours:** sops:weekly_review
- **routines_on:** weekly_review
- **routines_off:** ronin_worktrees

## agents

### review lead
- **team_lead:** yes
- **instructions:** Run the review: gather what the reader and the planner produce, keep it to one page, and put it in front of the owner. `+review:` is the whole procedure.
- **mandate:** execute · nobody · an artifact

### record reader
- **instructions:** Read every work record and document the week produced and report what moved, what stalled, and what was left half-done — facts, not judgement.
- **mandate:** execute · nobody · an artifact

### next week planner
- **instructions:** From the reader's report and the owner's standing goals, draft next week as at most seven lines the owner can strike or keep.
- **mandate:** plan · nobody · a plan
