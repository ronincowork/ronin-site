# weekly_review — how this house reviews a week

> Installed from the template library. Your own copy in the sops store (`ronin-store sops`
> → `weekly_review.md`) replaces this file whole — a default, not law.
> **Voice: agent.** Written for the session that runs the review, to follow itself.

A weekly review is one page. It is read on Monday morning by someone who was not in the
room, so it names things, it does not narrate them.

## The shape

1. **The week** — `tejun-week` gives the Monday and Sunday; put them in the heading.
2. **What moved** — one line per thing that finished or visibly advanced, each naming the
   document or record that proves it. No adjectives.
3. **What stalled** — one line per thing that was started and not moved, with the reason
   as the record states it, or "no reason recorded".
4. **Left half-done** — anything with an open leg on a work record. These are next week's
   first candidates.
5. **Next week** — at most seven lines. Each is something one session can finish. The
   owner strikes or keeps them; the review is not the plan until they have.

## The rules

- Read the records, not your memory. `read_tegami --session <name>` is the source; a
  session that kept no record is reported as such, not guessed at.
- One page. If it runs over, the review has become a report; cut the narration.
- Post nothing to the wipeboard. The review is a document on the ▧ Docs tab; `+show_file:`
  is how the owner reaches it.
